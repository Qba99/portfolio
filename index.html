Launch health of Polish-made games on Steam

What does a healthy first 90 days on Steam look like, and which early signals warn a publisher that the rating is going wrong?

  • Collected 754,026 reviews for 23 games through Steam's API and modelled them in PostgreSQL.
  • The score is effectively set in 2 days: days 0–1 predict the days 7–90 score to within 2.9 points, so the rolling score is the real early warning.
  • Players inside Steam's 2-hour refund window are 20 points less positive than everyone else.
  • Chinese players add about 6,500 extra negative reviews, driven by performance and servers, not translation.

Built with Python, PostgreSQL, Power BI (star schema, 19 DAX measures) and Tableau.

Cutting unplanned downtime on a machine fleet

Where do failures cost a plant most, do the machines warn before they fail, and would acting on those warnings pay off?

  • Per-machine control charts showed each part has its own warning sign about a day ahead.
  • A two-signal alert caught 97% of failures, tested on months it was never tuned on.
  • 86% of its alerts pointed to a real problem, with about 19 hours of notice.
  • An Excel business case puts the saving at 11.8 million PLN a year in the base case, and shows when it would not pay.

Built with PostgreSQL, Python and Excel (Power Query, Data Table, Scenario Manager).
